    Frequently asked questions

    How long does an installation take?

    Rooftop systems usually take a few days. Larger solar, wind and storage plants take weeks, depending on approvals.

    Do I need a battery with solar?

    No. A battery is optional, but it lets you use stored power in the evening and during outages.

    Is my site suitable for wind power?

    We study a year of wind data for your site first, because wind works best where speeds are steady.

    What maintenance is needed?

    Panels need periodic cleaning and inspection. Turbines and batteries follow a yearly service plan.
